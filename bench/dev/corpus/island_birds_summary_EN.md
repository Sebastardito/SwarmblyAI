# Inbreeding in the fictitious island mockingbird (Mimus ficticius) — summary (fictitious)

Populations of Mimus ficticius on the small island of Floresta show high inbreeding: runs of homozygosity cover about 18 % of the genome, against 4 % on the larger island of Verdemar.

The Floresta population fell to fewer than 60 adults after a drought in 2019. Hatching success was 41 % on Floresta and 68 % on Verdemar.
